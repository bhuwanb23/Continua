Continua Phase 13: Oncology Flagship Experience

Phase 12 freezes the real coordinator product. Phase 13 now creates the judge-facing experience on top of that product without falsifying rankings, fabricating outcomes, or turning Continua into a scripted prototype.

This phase is about one thing:

Make the problem emotionally obvious in 10 seconds, the product understandable in 30, the intelligence memorable in 60, and the trust/technical differentiation undeniable by 90.

The oncology vertical is our storytelling wedge. The underlying system remains general continuity intelligence.

Do not make cancer itself the emotional trick.

That's cheap and risky.

Much stronger.

13.6 The "Wow" Moment Needs Correction

Our original line was:

"Sarah reviewed Maria's chart for 12 minutes yesterday. She missed that Maria hadn't seen oncology in 45 days. Continua found it in 8 seconds."

We should not ship that wording unchanged.

There are three problems:

12 minutes may be only a scenario assumption.
8 seconds must be measured if used.
The records only prove a completed oncology consult wasn't found in the available data, not necessarily that Maria never saw oncology.
Safer final form

"Buried across Maria's records was an oncology referral whose follow-up window had passed, with no completed oncology consultation found in the available records. Continua surfaced it immediately for Sarah to verify."

If Phase 9 measured an appropriate latency, we can replace "immediately" with the measured number.

13.52 Top Five Expected Judge Questions
1. "Doesn't Epic already do this?"

Answer:

"Epic and health information exchanges help move and organize records. Continua targets the synthesis step after records arrive: identifying unresolved cross-provider coordination issues, grounding them to source evidence, and prioritizing them for coordinator review."

Do not claim Epic has zero similar functionality.

2. "Is this just an LLM wrapper?"

"No. The LLM performs only structured event extraction. Gap detection, evidence verification, and intervention priority are deterministic and independently evaluated."

Then methodology.

3. "How do you know it is right?"

"We evaluate against a frozen synthetic benchmark with planted ground truth, held-out patients, adversarial negative cases, and source-grounding checks."

Then actual metrics.

4. "Would you deploy this in a hospital?"

"Not as-is. This is a synthetic-data prototype. Real deployment would require EHR integration, security and privacy controls, clinical validation, governance, and prospective testing."

Strong answer.

5. "What does the coordinator actually do?"

"Continua surfaces the issue and supporting evidence. The coordinator verifies it and decides the workflow action. Continua doesn't diagnose or determine treatment."
