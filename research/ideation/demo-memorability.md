# Ideation Task 4 — Demo Memorability Analysis

## Part 1: Why Uber / Stripe / Notion / Duolingo / Linear Are Memorable

| Product | The "one thing" | Why it sticks |
|---|---|---|
| **Uber** | "Tap a button, get a ride." | One verb, one outcome. The product *is* the promise. No explanation needed; the demo is the product. |
| **Stripe** | "Payments infrastructure for the internet." | Category creation + audience ("for the internet"). The demo is "7 lines of code." Developer-first identity. |
| **Notion** | "One workspace. Every team." / "Write, plan, organize." | Flexibility as identity. The blank canvas IS the product; community templates do the marketing. |
| **Duolingo** | "Learn a language for free. Forever." | Gamification + streaks + the owl mascot. Emotional hook (shame/guilt/pride). The mascot *is* the brand. |
| **Linear** | "Issue tracking you'll enjoy using." / "Built for speed." | Opinionated, fast, beautiful. The *feel* is the differentiator. "Linear is fast" is the whole brand. |

### Five shared traits (the memorability formula)
1. **The tagline is a promise of an outcome, not a feature list.** ("get a ride," not "GPS-enabled dispatch platform.")
2. **One core interaction that is instantly demoable.** Tap → ride. 7 lines → payment. Blank page → doc.
3. **An emotional or identity hook.** Shame (Duolingo), pride (Linear speed), belonging (Notion community).
4. **A single unforgettable number or visual.** Uber's map with the car moving. Stripe's 7 lines. Linear's instant load.
5. **The name is a noun/verb that *is* the thing.** Uber = ride. Stripe = payment strip. Linear = straight line.

### The healthcare problem
"Care coordination" is abstract. "Continuity" is abstract. "Care gap" is jargon. None of these trigger an image. The product as currently framed ("AI Continuity Workbench") describes a *mechanism*, not a *moment*. It has no equivalent of "tap a button, get a ride."

### How to create the same immediate understanding for a healthcare project
We need all five traits:
1. **Outcome-promise tagline:** "Finds the patients most likely to fall through the cracks before they do." (already good — keep)
2. **One core interaction:** Open the workbench → see the 5 patients who need you today, ranked, with the reason and the one next action. That's the "tap → ride."
3. **Emotional/identity hook:** The coordinator who never misses a patient. Pride in catching what others missed. The "hero" moment: "Sarah reviewed this chart for 12 minutes and missed this. The system found it in 8 seconds."
4. **Unforgettable number/visual:** "80 patients → 5 need you today." The ranked queue with red/amber/green. The animated timeline. The "1 in 3 patients on Sarah's list has a missed follow-up she didn't know about."
5. **Name that *is* the thing:** needs work — see `naming-sprint.md`. The name should evoke the *moment* (finding, catching, closing), not the *mechanism* (continuity, workbench).

**The "wow" moment (the thing the judge tells their friends about):** The AI catches a gap the human *missed*, on a chart the coordinator already reviewed. Reveal it dramatically: "Sarah spent 12 minutes on this chart yesterday. She missed this. The system found it in 8 seconds." That is the Uber moment.
