# Ideation Task 5 — Product Naming Sprint

**Requirements:** Healthcare, professional, memorable, demo-friendly, can become a startup. Avoid: MediAI, HealthGPT, CareBot, and generic AI-suffix names.

**Naming strategy buckets:** (a) the *moment* (finding/catching/closing), (b) the *tool* (workbench/queue/radar), (c) the *outcome* (continuity/leakage/recovery), (d) abstract/evocative (one-word brandable).

---

## Bucket A — The Moment (finding / catching / closing)

1. **Catch** — "We catch the patients slipping through." One word, verb, memorable.
2. **Findr** — misspelled for brandability; "find" is the core action.
3. **Net** — "the safety net." Short, warm, metaphorical.
4. **Sieve** — filters 80 patients down to 5. Slightly clinical but distinctive.
5. **Scoop** — "scoop up the slipping patients." Friendly, action-oriented.
6. **Snag** — catches what others miss. Casual but punchy.
7. **Reel** — "reel them back in." Recovery metaphor.
8. **Hook** — "hook the ones slipping away." Strong, short.
9. **Lasso** — "lasso the patients falling through." Distinctive, slightly playful.
10. **Catchment** — clinical term for "area served"; repurposed as "catching patients."

## Bucket B — The Tool (workbench / queue / radar)

11. **Radar** — "patient radar." Universal, instantly understood.
12. **Triage** — clinical term; means "prioritize." Strong healthcare credibility.
13. **Queue** — "the coordinator's queue." Descriptive, honest.
14. **Console** — "coordinator console." Professional, tool-like.
15. **Cockpit** — "the coordinator's cockpit." Implies control and visibility.
16. **Desk** — "the coordinator's desk." Warm, human, non-techy.
17. **Board** — "the patient board." Simple, familiar (like a whiteboard).
18. **Lens** — "a lens on your panel." Implies clarity and focus.
19. **Scope** — "scope your panel." Implies seeing clearly.
20. **Pulse** — "the pulse of your panel." Vital-signs metaphor; warm.

## Bucket C — The Outcome (continuity / leakage / recovery)

21. **Continua** — "continuity, continuous." Latin root; brandable, professional.
22. **Continuum** — the unbroken care journey. Strong, clinical.
23. **CloseLoop** — "close the loop on every referral." Action-oriented.
24. **LoopBack** — "loop patients back into care." Warm, action-oriented.
25. **Recover** — "recover leaked patients." Direct ROI language.
26. **Recapture** — "recapture leaked referrals." Revenue language Jad loves.
27. **Retain** — "retain patients in care." Simple, business-flavored.
28. **Reconnect** — "reconnect patients to care." Human, warm.
29. **Bridge** — "bridge the gaps between providers." Universal metaphor.
30. **Thread** — "thread the patient journey together." Weaving metaphor; elegant.

## Bucket D — Abstract / Evocative (one-word brandable)

31. **Lumen** — "shed light on the panel." Light metaphor; premium feel.
32. **Vanta** — abstract, dark/premium; sounds like a serious tool.
33. **Aether** — "the space between records." Ethereal but professional.
34. **Nova** — "a new view of the panel." Bright, optimistic.
35. **Helix** — "the DNA of the care journey." Scientific, memorable.
36. **Axis** — "the axis of care." Structural, professional.
37. **Vector** — "direction for every patient." Mathematical, precise.
38. **Prism** — "refract the records into clarity." Light metaphor; distinctive.
39. **Beacon** — "a beacon for patients slipping away." Warm, guiding.
40. **Sentinel** — "standing watch over the panel." Guard metaphor; serious.

## Bucket E — Compound / Hybrid (two words, one brand)

41. **CareThread** — "thread the care journey." Healthcare + weaving.
42. **PatientRadar** — "radar for slipping patients." Clear, self-explanatory.
43. **GapClose** — "close the gaps." Direct, action-first.
44. **FollowUp** — "never miss a follow-up." Honest, plain-spoken.
45. **NextStep** — "the next right action." Outcome-first.
46. **CareLoop** — "close the care loop." Warm, circular metaphor.
47. **PanelPulse** — "the pulse of your panel." Alliteration; memorable.
48. **ChartIQ** — "intelligence for charts." (Avoids "AI" suffix; uses "IQ.")
49. **MedThread** — "thread the medical record." Clinical + weaving.
50. **CareCompass** — "compass for care coordination." Guiding metaphor; professional.

---

## Top Recommendations (for the decision gate)

- **Best all-rounder:** **Continua** (professional, brandable, implies unbroken care, can be a startup name).
- **Best "moment" name:** **Catch** (one word, verb, memorable, the "wow" line writes itself: "Catch found what Sarah missed.").
- **Best "tool" name:** **Triage** (instant healthcare credibility; means "prioritize," which is the product).
- **Best "outcome" name:** **CloseLoop** (Jad-friendly ROI language; "we close the loop on every referral").
- **Best "brandable" name:** **Lumen** (premium, distinctive, not generic).

**Naming principle:** The name should make the "wow" line work. Test: *"____ found what Sarah missed in 8 seconds."* If the name sounds natural in that sentence, it's a candidate. **Catch, Continua, Triage, CloseLoop, Lumen** all pass. **MediAI, HealthGPT, CareBot** do not.
